# Explain how NoSwipe operates dating apps

**Problem.** The site promises to swipe, chat, and book dates. The FAQ does not explain that the service uses phones it operates, or what the client sees after the service acts.

**Scope.** Update the shared public FAQ. Engine, onboarding, and privacy changes depend on confirming who performs each action and the account and data lifecycle.

**Options.** Keep the current general promise or explain the confirmed operating model. Use the latter so customers can evaluate the service before signing up.

**Approach.** Explain phone-based operation, reports on chats and matches, date planning, and account restriction risk. Avoid promises about testing, ban rates, and who performs each individual action until verified.

**Risk.** Account access and message visibility affect customers' privacy expectations. Review the wording against the actual intake and reporting flow before release.
